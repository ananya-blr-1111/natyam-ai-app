## Full description (4000 char max)
```
Natyam AI gives Bharatanatyam students instant, AI-powered feedback on their dance — built by a dancer, for dancers.

Upload a short video of your practice or performance, and Natyam AI analyses:

• POSTURE & MUDRA — body alignment, stance and hand gestures against correct form
• ABHINAYA — facial expression and how clearly your emotion (bhava) comes across
• LAYA — rhythmic precision and timing, and where footwork drifts off-beat

You'll get a score out of 100, a breakdown of what's working, what needs attention, and your top 3 things to practise next — all in under a minute.

WHO IT'S FOR
Whether you're learning your first adavus or refining your abhinaya ahead of an Arangetram, Natyam AI offers guidance at every level, any time you don't have a teacher in the room.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No login, no account, no cloud storage. Every video is processed on your own phone, and only a few still frames are sent securely for analysis — never the video itself. Your results are saved privately on your device only. Full privacy policy: https://natyam.dance/ai-app/privacy.html

BUILT BY A DANCER
Natyam AI is designed and built by Ananya Sharma, a Bharatanatyam dancer who completed her own Arangetram in 2026 after ten years of training. Learn more about her at https://natyam.dance

This is an early release — feedback and bug reports are very welcome.
```
